# Project context

We just joined the cash management team of a retail bank. Our manager has two
questions about one of the bank's ATMs. First, how much cash will customers
withdraw each day over the next 28 days? We answer with time series
forecasting. Second, how many cash cassettes should we load each day to keep
costs low without the ATM running empty? We answer with reinforcement
learning. The two parts are tested separately on simulated data, so we do not
claim they work together or on a real ATM network.
